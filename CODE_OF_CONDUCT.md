# Code of conduct

Be respectful and keep discussion focused on the project. Disagreement is
fine; harassment, threats, personal attacks, discrimination and publishing
someone else's private information are not.

Project spaces include issues, pull requests, reviews and other discussions
connected to this repository. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content
that disrupts the project or puts someone's privacy at risk. Repeated or
serious misconduct may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct problems privately through the repository's private
vulnerability reporting form. Include links or screenshots needed to explain
the incident, but do not repost sensitive material publicly.
